## Talking points

- The opening story is the lesson in miniature: a grader with no definition scored the worst traces a 9.
- Humans first is sequencing, not anti-automation. L5 automates the modes this lesson names.
- The benevolent dictator is a role. One person merges notes and settles boundaries so the taxonomy stays sharp. At Pip's it is Maya, because she knows what correct means.
- The four open-coding rules in order: whole trace, first failure, plain language, no categories. Most students break rule four by the third trace.
- The boundary column is the product of axial coding. Without it, two labelers will argue in L5.
- Saturation is a stopping rule for this sample, not a claim about the agent. Rare modes arrive late, so sample by tag too.

## Live demo idea

Project five traces from the smoke run, including one pass and one adversarial. Give the class two minutes per trace to write a first-failure note in a shared sheet, then read the notes aloud. Wording will vary and evidence will agree, which is the point. Sort the notes into piles live, name each pile with a verb phrase, and write one boundary together for the pile with the most disagreement. Twelve minutes.

## Common misconceptions

- "Start with a list of categories." You will find only what you expected. The data leak in tr-0e4 was on no list.
- "Note everything wrong in the trace." Downstream errors are consequences. First failure only; full labeling happens in L5.
- "Wrong tone needs a 1 to 5 scale." Binary with a boundary. Scales destroy agreement and cannot be validated.
- "Five modes after twenty traces is done." The two most dangerous modes arrived in batch two.
- "Published taxonomies save time." They anchor you. Gap check only, after your own exists.

## If you only have 20 minutes

Tell the opening story (2 minutes). Open-code three of the six traces with the class, enforcing first failure and plain language (8 minutes). Show the taxonomy table and read the Refund outside policy and Ignored tool error boundaries aloud, including which wins when both apply (6 minutes). End with the saturation table and the rule: stop when a batch adds nothing (4 minutes). Skip the sampling code and the gap check.
