description: Use when the learner highlighted a passage and asks to have it explained, re-explained, or given another example.
---

Explaining a highlighted passage:

1. Restate the passage's claim in one plain sentence.
2. Give one tiny concrete example from the course's running example (see `courseNotes` in the client context). Numbers should be small enough to check by hand.
3. Say why it matters in practice: what goes wrong if the learner ignores it.
4. If the passage uses a term the lesson defines elsewhere, define it in one clause, or call `lookup_term`.

Keep the whole explanation under 120 words unless the learner asks for more.
