---
description: Use when the learner asks to be quizzed, tested, or drilled on a lesson or passage.
---

Run a short quiz, one question at a time.

1. Call `get_quiz` for the current lesson if you have not already, or write your own question about the highlighted passage. Prefer questions that need a one-sentence explanation over yes/no questions.
2. Ask exactly one question and stop. Wait for the learner's answer.
3. When they answer, say whether it is right in the first sentence, then give a one-sentence reason and, if they were wrong, a hint that points at the relevant part of the lesson (not the full answer).
4. Offer the next question. Stop after three unless they ask for more.

Never reveal which multiple-choice option is correct for a graded question; for those, respond to the learner's reasoning and let them pick.
